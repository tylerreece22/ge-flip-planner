How it works

Every item has two live prices. The instant-sell price is what impatient sellers accept, so a buy offer placed there gets filled as people dump. The instant-buy price is what impatient buyers pay, so a sell offer placed there gets filled as people buy. All prices here come from real trades recorded by the OSRS Wiki.

The Grand Exchange takes 2% of every sale, so the break-even sell price is the buy price divided by 0.98. Every profit shown is after that tax.

The three kinds of trade

  • Packing a set. Bid for the pieces, pack them at a Grand Exchange clerk (right-click, Sets), and sell the set. Buyers pay extra for a set in one click, while people dumping drops sell single pieces cheaply.
  • Unpacking a set. The reverse: buy the set, unpack it at the clerk, and sell the pieces.
  • Plain flip. Buy at the instant-sell price and sell at the instant-buy price. Margins are thinner, so these mostly fill leftover slots.

The checks every option has to pass

  1. The gap has to persist. Profit is worked out separately for the last 4 hours, 4–12 hours ago and 12–24 hours ago. A gap in all three is structural; a gap in only one is usually a single odd trade and is left out of your plan.
  2. It has to actually trade. Quantities are capped by the buy limit and by half of what the market trades between your check-ins, counted on the slowest side.
  3. Spikes are priced in. If the thing you would sell is more than 4% above its 7-day price, the card says what you would make if it dropped back.
  4. Plain flips must be calm. An item moving more than 5% against its week, or 3% within the day, is not offered as a flip.
  5. Slots count. A four-piece set needs four slots to buy, so options are ranked by what fits the slots and cash you enter.
  6. Return has a floor. Your plan only uses trades whose profit after tax is at least the minimum return you pick (8% unless you change it). Among those it chooses the combination with the most profit. This keeps your cash out of large low-margin trades that only win on size.

Choosing your own

Click any column heading in the option list to sort by it, for example by return. Tick the options you want and the plan is rebuilt from just those, with cash going to the highest return first. Your own picks are sized by the same volume and buy-limit caps, but the minimum return is not applied to them.

How the offer prices are set

  • Buy offers sit just above where dumps have actually been filling over the last few hours.
  • Sell offers sit just under the cheapest recent seller, and never above the last six hours' median.

How fresh it is

Prices are pulled live each time you open the page. The 24-hour and 10-day history behind the checks is rebuilt every 4 hours. The line at the top right shows both.

Nothing you type here leaves your browser. Cash, slots and hidden options are remembered on this device only.
